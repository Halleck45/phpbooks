# Shipping Multi-Language, Multi-Site

One codebase, several languages, several national subsidiaries with slightly different content: a request that sounds like a translation problem and is usually, underneath, a content architecture problem. Getting the structure right (how a translated page relates to its original, who can edit what) matters more than the translation mechanism itself.

- [TYPO3: Multilingual Content Trees Done Properly](ch13-01-typo3-multilingual-trees.md) covers a content structure built for this from the start.
- [WordPress: Multisite and WPML](ch13-02-wordpress-multisite-wpml.md) covers two different WordPress answers to two different versions of this problem.
- [Symfony: The Translation Component](ch13-03-symfony-translation-component.md) covers translating an application's own interface, not its content.
