# Shipping a Content Site

Marketing pages, blogs, documentation, portfolios: the value is the content, and someone non-technical will update it after launch. Writing a custom application for this is almost always the wrong call. **Every option in this chapter exists so that you never build an editor, a content model, or a publishing workflow from scratch.**

<img src="images/ch03-editable-page.png" alt="A large page pinned to a wall, divided into blocks. A person moves one block to a new place with both hands while a small elephant holds a painter's palette with only three colors on it" width="560">

The four options trade "fastest to a first page" against "best fit for a large, structured, or long-lived site" in different ways.

- [WordPress: Block Themes and the Site Editor](ch03-01-wordpress-block-themes.md): the fastest path to an editable site, and the one that runs the largest share of the content web.
- [TYPO3: Structured Content at Enterprise Scale](ch03-02-typo3-structured-content.md): for when "content" means thousands of structured pages across departments, not one blog.
- [Statamic ($ for commercial use): A Flat-File CMS Built on Laravel](ch03-03-statamic-flat-file-cms.md): WordPress-like editing without a database, for Laravel developers.
- [Craft CMS ($): A Licensed CMS Built for Editorial Control](ch03-04-craft-cms.md): a bespoke content model, for clients willing to pay a commercial license to get it.
