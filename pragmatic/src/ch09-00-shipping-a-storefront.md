# Shipping a Storefront

Products, a cart, checkout, and taking real payments without accidentally storing a credit card number somewhere you shouldn't. E-commerce is one of the areas where "just build it yourself" is rarely the pragmatic answer: taxes, refunds, inventory, and payment compliance all carry real risk if handled casually.

- [WooCommerce: E-Commerce on Top of WordPress](ch09-01-woocommerce.md) is the fastest path when the site is already, or should be, WordPress.
- [Sylius: A Symfony-Based E-Commerce Framework](ch09-02-sylius.md) fits a custom storefront with real business logic, built on Symfony's component ecosystem.
- [Laravel: Cashier and Stripe ($) for Custom Checkouts](ch09-03-laravel-cashier-stripe.md) fits a Laravel app that just needs a subscription or checkout flow, not a full store.
