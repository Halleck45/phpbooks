# Choosing Your Stack in Five Minutes

Before any feature ships, one decision shapes everything downstream: what kind of thing you are building, and what already exists that gets you most of the way there. **Get this right and every later chapter becomes a menu you order from.** Get it wrong and you spend six months fighting a tool that was never meant to build what you are building.

<img src="images/ch01-three-doors.png" alt="Three closed doors, each marked with an icon: gears, a pencil over a page, a plug and socket. A person reads a single sheet of paper in front of them while a small elephant points at the middle door" width="560">

This chapter is short on purpose. It gives you a decision guide, not a religion, and a way to have something running in under a minute, so you can feel whether the choice was right before you have spent an afternoon on it.

- [Framework, CMS, or Headless: What Are You Actually Building?](ch01-01-framework-cms-or-headless.md): the three shapes a project takes, and which one your brief describes.
- [Scaffolding a First Project in Under a Minute](ch01-02-scaffolding-a-first-project.md): the command to run for each main option in this book, so you compare them hands-on instead of from a chart.
